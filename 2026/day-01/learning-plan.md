#### **What is your understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering?**

**Ans:-**  
 * **DevOps** is a culture and set of practices that improves collaboration between Development and Operations teams.

 * It helps organizations build, test, and deploy applications faster and more efficiently.

 * DevOps uses automation to reduce manual work and minimize errors.

 * **Cloud Engineering** involves designing, deploying, and managing applications and infrastructure on cloud platforms.

 * Cloud Engineering helps organizations reduce infrastructure costs and improve availability.

 * Cloud Engineers work with services related to compute, storage, networking, security, and monitoring.
